# Product

## Register

product

## Users

You and a small, trusted circle of people. Not a broad public audience. Users are comfortable with dense, capable tools and have no patience for being hand-held. They open LifeHub to track something, log something, or review something, and they want that task done in seconds without ceremony.

## Product Purpose

LifeHub is an all-in-one personal life management hub: expense tracking, gym workouts, note-taking, AI assistant, habits, daily routine, and receipt scanning. The goal isn't feature breadth for its own sake — it's having everything in one place so context-switching disappears. Success looks like a daily-use tool that feels faster and quieter every time you open it.

## Brand Personality

Focused. Powerful. Quietly opinionated.

No cheerfulness, no motivational copy, no onboarding hand-holding. The product respects that the user already knows what they want to do.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS**: Notion-clone aesthetics, pastel cards, rounded-everything, hero metrics with gradient accents, identical icon-heading-text card grids. LifeHub should feel nothing like this category.
- No playful illustration, mascots, or emoji-driven empty states.
- No enterprise blue, navy-gold finance clichés, or fitness neon.

## References

**Linear** — precise interaction model, keyboard-first feel, information presented without decorative scaffolding, dark UI that feels native rather than "dark mode as feature."

## Design Principles

1. **No wasted pixels.** Every element earns its place. No decorative padding, no filler subheadings that restate the page title, no visual chrome that doesn't carry information.
2. **Quiet confidence.** The interface doesn't celebrate itself. Data is the hero; the shell recedes. Violet accent exists to direct attention, not to perform personality.
3. **Density without chaos.** More information on screen is better than less, as long as hierarchy is clear. Group and tier; never flatten everything to the same visual weight.
4. **Precision over warmth.** Interactions respond immediately and exactly. Animations serve orientation, not delight. No bounce, no elastic, no celebratory flourishes.
5. **Personal, not personalized.** The interface knows its audience without constantly announcing it. Familiarity comes from consistency and speed, not from greeting the user by name on every screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Good contrast ratios and keyboard-navigable interactions. No formal WCAG level required, but text must be readable at standard dark-theme contrast and interactive elements must be reachable without a mouse.
